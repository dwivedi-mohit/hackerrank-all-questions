## Preview

Calculate the slope of a regression line.

## Problem Statement

**Objective** <br>
In this challenge, we practice calculating the slope of regression lines.

**Task** <br>
There are $2$ series of data involving index numbers: $P$ for price index and $S$ for the commodity stock. <br>
 The mean and standard deviation of $P$ are $100$ and $8$, respectively.<br> 
 The mean and standard deviation of $S$ are $103$ and $4$, respectively. <br>
 The $R^2$ correlation coefficient between the two series is $0.4$. 
 
With this data, obtain the *slope* of the regression line of $P$ on $S$, correct to a scale of $2$ decimal places.
