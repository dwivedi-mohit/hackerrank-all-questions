## Problem Statement

You are provided the following image $B$

	0000000000
    0111111100
    0000111100
    0000111100
    0001111100
    0000111100
    0001100000
    0000000000
    0000000000
  

The structuring element $S$ is given below, and its origin is the middle pixel.

	111
	111
	111
  

What is the number of pixels marked 1 in the image obtained after $B$ is eroded with the structuring element $S$? 
Enter the appropriate integer in the text box below.

Alternatively, you may submit a program which computes and displays the required integer.
