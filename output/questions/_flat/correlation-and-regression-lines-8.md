## Problem Statement

Here are the test scores of 10 students in physics and history:

    Physics Scores	15	12	8	8	7	7	7	6	5	3
	History	Scores	10  25	17	11	13	17	20	13	9	15
 
  

When a student scores 10 in Physics, what is his probable score in History? 
Compute the answer correct to one decimal place.


**Output Format**


In the text box, enter the floating point/decimal value required. Do not leave any leading or trailing spaces. Your answer may look like:
	5.5
  

This is **NOT** the actual answer - just the format in which you should provide your answer.
