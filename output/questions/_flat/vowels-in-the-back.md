## Preview

Recognize strings with vowels at specific positions.

## Problem Statement

In this problem, we want to recognize **lowercase strings with vowels as their $10^{th}$ letter from the back**.

Your task is to write a regular expression identifying a string, $S$ with the following conditions:
 
 - $S$ has a length of, at least, $10$.
 - $S$ consists only of lowercase English letters.
 - $S$ has a vowel as its $10^{th}$ letter from the back. We define vowels as: $\{a, e, i, o, u, y\}$ here.
