## Preview

## Problem Statement

**Objective**	
In this challenge, we practice calculating probability. Check out the [Tutorial](/challenges/s10-mcq-1/tutorial) tab for a breakdown of probability fundamentals!
<br><br>

**Task**	
In a single toss of $2$ fair (evenly-weighted) six-sided dice, find the probability that their sum will be *at most* $9$.
