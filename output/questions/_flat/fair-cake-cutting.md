## Preview

Find the number of people to invite so that each person receives a particular amount of cake.

## Problem Statement

Leha is a young man from Belarus. He loves cakes really much. In Leha's country, cakes are always evenly divided between people.

Leha wants to know how many people he should invite to his party if he has a cake whose size is equal to $a$ units and he wants each person to get *exactly* $\frac{A}{B}$ units of cake. Note that Leha doesn't take a piece for himself; in other words, the cake is only divided among the people he invited to the party.

## Input Format

The first line contains three space-separated integers describing the respective values of $A$, $B$, and $a$.

## Output Format

Print a single integer denoting $b$, the number of people he should invite to his party.

## Constraints

- $1 \le A, B \le 10^9$

- $1 \le a \le A$

- It is guaranteed that the answer is an integer.
