## Preview

Given two regression lines, compute the co-variance of $y$.

## Problem Statement

The two regression lines of a bivariate distribution are:
 4$x$ – 5$y$ + 33 = 0 and 20$x$ – 9$y$ – 107 = 0.
find the variance of y when $σx$ = 3.
Compute the correct answer to one decimal place.

In the text box, enter the floating point or decimal value required. Do not lead any leading or trailing spaces. Your answer may look like:
