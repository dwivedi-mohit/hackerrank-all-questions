## Preview

Split the string by the pattern occurrence using the re.split() expression.

## Problem Statement

<sub>Check [Tutorial](https://www.hackerrank.com/challenges/re-split/tutorial) tab to know how to to solve.</sub>


You are given a string $s$ consisting only of digits `0-9`, commas `,`, and dots `.`

Your task is to complete the `regex_pattern` defined below, which will be used to *re.split()* all of the `,` and `.` symbols in $s$.

It’s guaranteed that every comma and every dot in $s$ is preceeded and followed by a digit.
