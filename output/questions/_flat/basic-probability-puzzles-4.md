## Problem Statement

**Objective** <br>
In this challenge, we practice calculating probability. 

**Task** <br>
$Bag_1$ contains $4$ red balls and $5$ black balls. <br>
$Bag_2$ contains $3$ red balls and $7$ black balls. <br> 

One ball is drawn from the $Bag_1$, and $2$ balls are drawn from $Bag_2$. Find the probability that $2$ balls are black and $1$ ball is red.

## Output Format

In the editor below, submit your answer as *Plain Text* in the form of an irreducible fraction $A/B$, where $A$ and $B$ are both integers. 

Your answer should resemble something like:
	
    3/4
