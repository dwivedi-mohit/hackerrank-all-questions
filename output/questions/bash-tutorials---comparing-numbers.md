## Problem Statement

Given two integers, $X$ and $Y$, identify whether $X \lt Y$ or $X \gt Y$ or $X = Y$.   


Exactly one of the following lines:   
- _X is less than Y_   
- _X is greater than Y_   
- _X is equal to Y_ 

## Input Format

Two lines containing one integer each ($X$ and $Y$, respectively).  


## Output Format

Exactly one of the following lines:   
- _X is less than Y_   
- _X is greater than Y_   
- _X is equal to Y_
