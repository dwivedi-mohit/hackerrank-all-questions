## Problem Statement

**Objective** <br>
In this challenge, we practice solving problems with normally distributed variables.

**Task** <br>
$X$ is a normally distributed variable with a mean of $μ = 30$ and a standard deviation of $σ = 4$. Find:

- $P(x < 40)$ 
- $P(x > 21)$ 
- $P(30 < x < 35)$
