## Preview

Query the Western Longitude for the smallest value of the Northern Latitudes greater than 38.7780 in STATION and round to 4 decimal places.

## Problem Statement

Query the *Western Longitude* (*LONG\_W*)where the smallest *Northern Latitude* (*LAT\_N*) in **STATION** is greater than $38.7780$. Round your answer to $4$ decimal places.
