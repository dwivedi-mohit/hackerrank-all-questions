## Problem Statement

A globally distributed team in a tech company uses Grafana dashboards to monitor their applications across multiple environments. Recently, certain critical visualizations in a shared dashboard occasionally display outdated data points, even though the underlying data source is updated. The anomaly is only seen in the Grafana dashboard, not in the data source's UI. Their company wants to determine the root cause of this behavior and ensure the real-time accuracy of its dashboards.

Which of the following steps should be taken first to diagnose and address the issue?
