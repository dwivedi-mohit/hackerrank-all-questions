## Problem Statement

In natural language processing, stop words are frequently used words such as "a", "is", and "the" that are usually filtered out during text processing.

 

Write a function that accepts a string text and an integer k, and returns a list of words that appear at least k times in the text. The words should be returned in the order of their first occurrence in the text.

 

Example

Suppose text = "a mouse is smaller than a dog but a dog is stronger" and k=2

Output: ["a", "is", "dog"]

Explanation:

In order of appearance, the words that occur at least k = 2 times are "a", "is", and "dog". 

 

Constraints

	
- 
text has at most 50000 characters.
	
- Every character in text is either an English lowercase letter or a space.
	
- 
text starts and ends with a letter. No two consecutive characters are spaces, i.e., text is a valid sentence.
	
- There is at least one stop word in text.
	
- 1 ≤ k ≤ 18
