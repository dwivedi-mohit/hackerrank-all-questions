## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates data optimization, database performance, and Master Data Management concepts, ideal for mid-level roles. The problem requires selecting an MDM strategy to improve page load times and ensure customer data privacy on an e-commerce platform.

## Problem Statement

Customers on an e-commerce website face slow page loads on their daily deals page due to database performance issues. Also, personal customer information is being retrieved from cookies without their consent.

 

Which Master Data Management (MDM) strategy should be implemented to address these issues?
