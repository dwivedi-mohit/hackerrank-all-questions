## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates storage solutions, scalability, and cost-efficiency concepts, ideal for mid-level roles. The problem requires proposing tailored storage solutions for three different business scenarios at an IT conference.

## Problem Statement

You are at an IT conference, and you overhear a conversation among three business representatives, they are talking about storage solutions and are confused about how to move forward with a solution for their business. Business Rep 1 runs an SMB that is cost-efficient, Rep 2 is an IT manager for a business that is doing acquisitions of other business' fast and need to scale their overall IT solutions. Rep 3 has a bakery with 2 locations and needs a better way to store his data.

 

You introduce yourself to the Reps and start to present a solution to each of their problems. What would your solution look like?

 

Choose all that apply.
