## Problem Statement

A new electric vehicle (EV) model is being developed by a major automotive manufacturing company. Different teams are working on different parts of the EV development process, including design, engineering and testing. In order to ensure a consistent pace of progress and deliverables on time, the company uses an agile framework known as 'Scaled Agile Framework' (SAFe). In this framework, teams are organized into agile release trains (ART).

How does Agile Release Train (ART) Work in a large automotive manufacturing company?
