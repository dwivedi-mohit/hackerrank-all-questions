## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates Azure networking, hybrid cloud integration, and connectivity solutions concepts, ideal for senior-level roles. The problem requires determining the optimal approach for connecting a complex on-premises network to Azure virtual networks.

## Problem Statement

A Fortune 500 company wishes to transition to a hybrid cloud model, combining its substantial on-premises infrastructure with the flexibility and scalability of Azure. To do so, they plan to establish connectivity between their on-premises data centers and Azure virtual networks.

 

They have multiple VLANs with a complex routing structure managed through a combination of physical routers and firewalls. They also use a variety of network appliances for security, WAN optimization, and other network functions. The company has several proprietary applications that rely on multicast for communication, which cannot be rearchitected in the near future.

 

What is the optimal approach to configure the connectivity from their on-premises network to Azure considering the current networking setup and constraints?
