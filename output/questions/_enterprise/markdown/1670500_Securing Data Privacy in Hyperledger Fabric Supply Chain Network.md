## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates Hyperledger Fabric, data privacy, and channel management concepts, ideal for junior-level roles. The problem requires determining actions to ensure data privacy between two organizations in a supply chain application.

## Problem Statement

In a Hyperledger Fabric network for a Supply Chain application involving three companies (OrgA, OrgB, and OrgC), transactions are channeled for grouping. However, OrgA wishes to exchange information exclusively with OrgB, excluding OrgC from the process.

 

What actions should be taken to ensure data privacy in this scenario?
