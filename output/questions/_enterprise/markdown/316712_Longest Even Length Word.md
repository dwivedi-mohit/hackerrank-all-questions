## Problem Statement

Find the length of the longest even length word in a sentence.

You are given a sentence.

Your task is to:

	
- Find the longest word in the sentence that has an even number of letters.
	
- If more than one word has the same longest even length, return the first one that appears in the sentence.
	
- If there is no even length word, return '00'.

 

Example

Suppose you are given the string (sentence): "Time to write great code"

Output: "Time"

 

The lengths of the words are 4, 2, 5, 5, and 4, respectively. The longest even-length words are "Time" and "code". Since "Time" appears first, that is the correct answer.

 

Constraints

	
- 1 ≤ length of sentence ≤ 105
	
- The sentence string consists of spaces and letters in the range ascii[a-z, A-Z, ] only.
