## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates PID controllers, system stability, and response optimization concepts, ideal for junior-level roles. The problem requires understanding the effects of increasing proportional gain on the response of a buck converter in a medical device application.

## Problem Statement

An electrical engineer must optimize a buck converter that powers a sensitive medical device. The engineer must ensure a quick response to load changes without causing instability in the device's power supply. During testing, the engineer considers adjusting the proportional gain of the PID controller used in the converter.

 

What could be the effect of increasing the proportional gain on the system's response to sudden load changes?
