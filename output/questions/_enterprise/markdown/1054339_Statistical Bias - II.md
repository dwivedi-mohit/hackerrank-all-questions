## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates statistical bias concepts, ideal for junior-level roles. The problem requires identifying the type of bias present in a company's demand estimation for umbrellas based on survey data from a rainy month.

## Problem Statement

"A company needs to estimate the demand for umbrellas. They conducted a survey in a rainy month and assumed the total demand for a year to be 12 times of that."

Which type of statistical bias is evident in this example?
