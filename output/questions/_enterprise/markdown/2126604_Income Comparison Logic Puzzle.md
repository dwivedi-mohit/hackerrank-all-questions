## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates logical reasoning, aptitude, and problem-solving concepts, ideal for junior-level roles. The problem requires determining who earns the highest income based on given income relationships among four individuals.

## Problem Statement

The sum of the income of A and B is greater than the sum of the income of C and D.
The sum of the income of A and C is equal to the sum of the income of B and D.
Moreover, A earns half as much as the sum of the income of B and D.

Based on the information above, who earns the highest income?
