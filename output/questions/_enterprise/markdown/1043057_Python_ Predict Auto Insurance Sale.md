## Problem Statement

Predict Auto Insurance Purchases

CarProtect is one of the leading auto insurance companies in the USA. They insure against financial loss from numerous risks such as accidents, theft and bodily injury. This year, the company has launched a new coverage plan for their existing customers. The management is interested in knowing which customers are more likely to buy this plan and what factors influence the results.

 

 CarProtect has provided you with a dataset of the customers whom they have reached out to consisting of their customer's data such as income, vehicle, education, present policy information and whether the customer purchased the new plan.

 Using machine learning, can you identify which customers will buy their new plan and identify how different features influence their decision?
