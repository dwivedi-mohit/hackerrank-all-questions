## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates logical reasoning, problem-solving, and analytical skills concepts, ideal for senior-level roles. The problem requires deducing the number of sections taught by Dr. George based on various constraints related to subject sections and teacher assignments.

## Problem Statement

Louis College London offers six subjects: math, chemistry, physics, botany, zoology and anatomy. Each subject has its own teacher, and each teacher teaches a different number of sections from 1 - 6. Given the following information, how many sections does Dr. George teach?

 

	
- Dr. Mason teaches 4 sections.
	
- Anatomy has 5 sections.
	
- Dr. Smith teaches 2 more sections than Dr. Noah. Neither teaches botany nor zoology.
	
- Dr. Thomas teaches less than 6 sections of physics.
	
- There are more sections of chemistry than math. Dr. Lucas does not teach chemistry.
	
- There are 3 more sections of zoology than botany.
