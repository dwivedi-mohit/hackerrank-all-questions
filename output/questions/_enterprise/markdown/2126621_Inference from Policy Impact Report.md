## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates inference, logical reasoning, and analytical skills, ideal for junior-level roles. The problem requires determining the most reasonable inference from a passage about the effects of congestion pricing on urban transportation.

## Problem Statement

Passage:

A recent government report on urban transportation policy noted that in cities where congestion pricing was implemented (charging vehicles for entering high-traffic zones during peak hours), traffic congestion decreased by 30% on average within the first year. Additionally, there was a measurable shift towards public transportation and cycling in those cities. However, the report also observed a 12% increase in ride-share vehicle presence in these zones, partially offsetting the congestion benefits. Despite this, overall average commute times were reduced. Some critics argue that congestion pricing disproportionately affects lower-income drivers, although subsidy programs for public transport were expanded simultaneously.

 

Question:
Which of the following can be most reasonably inferred from the passage?
