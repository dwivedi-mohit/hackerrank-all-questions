## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates algebra, speed, and relative motion concepts, ideal for junior-level roles. The problem requires calculating the length of a train based on its speed and the time taken to pass another train.

## Problem Statement

A train, 270 meters long, moving at 120 km/hour passes another train moving in the opposite direction at 80 km/hour in 9 seconds. What is the length of the train moving at 80 km/hour?
