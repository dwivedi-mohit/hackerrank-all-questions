## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates automation tools, integration processes, and marketing campaign strategies, ideal for mid-level roles. The problem assesses the appropriateness of using Workato for automating Zoom invitations and Slack notifications for a marketing campaign.

## Problem Statement

A project leader is planning a marketing campaign. The leader wishes to set up an automation that involves sending Zoom meeting invitations via Gmail to project stakeholders whose email addresses are stored in a Google Sheets document. They also want to notify the campaign team through Slack for a demo presentation on the meeting day.

 

From a marketing process automation perspective, is the solution appropriate?
