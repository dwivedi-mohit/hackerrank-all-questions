## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates outliers, mean, and median concepts, ideal for mid-level roles. The problem requires analyzing how extreme values in a dataset impact the mean and median in a normally distributed dataset.

## Problem Statement

Given a dataset 'data' with a large number of observations, the user is tasked with determining the impact of outliers on the dataset's mean and median. The dataset follows a roughly normal distribution but with a few extreme values on the higher end. How would these outliers likely affect the mean and median of the dataset?
