## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates number properties and divisibility concepts, ideal for mid-level roles. The problem requires determining how many integers from 1 to 100 meet specific conditions related to divisibility and digit presence.

## Problem Statement

For all integers from 1 to 100, how many match at least one of the following?

 

not divisible by 5 and contains the digit 5 

divisible by 5 but does not contain the digit 5
