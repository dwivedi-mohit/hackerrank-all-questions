## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates logical reasoning, problem-solving, and spatial arrangement concepts, ideal for mid-level roles. The problem requires determining the rightmost position of individuals based on given statements about their arrangement.

## Problem Statement

There are five people - Alex, Asher, Riley, Harper, and Jordan - standing in a row. Given the following statements, who is on the rightmost position?

1. Alex is next to Jordan, and Harper is next to Riley.

2. Harper is not next to Asher.

3. Asher is in the leftmost position.

4. Riley is in the second position from the right.

5. Alex stands somewhere to the right of both Jordan and Asher.

6. Alex and Riley are next to each other.
