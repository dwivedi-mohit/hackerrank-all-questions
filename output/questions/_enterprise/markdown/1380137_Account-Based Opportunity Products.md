## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates Salesforce Price Books, custom fields, and automation concepts, ideal for mid-level roles. The problem requires determining how to restrict product prices based on account type during the addition of products to Opportunity records.

## Problem Statement

A business has the following setup in Salesforce.

	
- On the Account object, there is a custom picklist field with the name Customer_Type__c with two possible values:

	
		
- New
		
- Existing
	
	
	
- There are two Price Books in Salesforce.
	
		
- New Customer Price
		
- Existing Customer Price
	
	
	
- All of the Products are available in both Price Books with different prices set by the business.
	
- As per the business process, it is required that the Account Type as New should be provided the Products with the price mentioned in the New Customer Price Price Book, and the Account Type as Existing should be provided the Products with the price in the Existing Customer Price price book.

The business requirement is to be able to select only applicable product prices while adding products into the Opportunity Product records from the standard Opportunity page Add Products button.

 

How can this be achieved?
