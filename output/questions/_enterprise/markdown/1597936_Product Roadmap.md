## Problem Statement

In HackSoft, a product manager has collected customer and stakeholder feedback and identified several potential areas for improvement, such as improved collaboration capabilities, mobile app integrations, and more advanced reporting. However, customer priorities are shifting rapidly due to market changes, and the competitive landscape is evolving. To lead the team's efforts effectively, what approach would be most effective for product roadmap development in this situation?
