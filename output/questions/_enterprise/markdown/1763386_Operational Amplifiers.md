## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates operational amplifier performance, Common-Mode Rejection Ratio (CMRR), and AC gain concepts, ideal for mid-level roles. The problem requires calculating the output common-mode voltage and AC gain based on given specifications.

## Problem Statement

A new line of operational amplifiers is being developed by an audio equipment manufacturer. During a critical design review, there is a need to verify the performance specifications of an operational amplifier whose design parameters include a Common-Mode Rejection Ratio (CMRR) of 100 dB, an input common-mode voltage of 12 V, a differential voltage gain of 4000, a unity gain bandwidth (UGB) of 10 MHz, and an open-loop gain (AOL) of 2×106. Based on this scenario, calculate the output common-mode voltage (VOCM) based on the CMRR and input parameters.

 

What is the AC gain value of the operational amplifier if the input frequency is 2000 Hz.?
