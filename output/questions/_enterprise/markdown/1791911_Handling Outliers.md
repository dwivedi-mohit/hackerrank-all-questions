## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates statistics, data analysis, and central tendency concepts, ideal for mid-level roles. The problem requires identifying the most suitable measure of central tendency for a dataset with outliers affecting the mean.

## Problem Statement

Given a dataset of customer ages for a large retail chain, the user is tasked with identifying the most suitable measure of central tendency to represent the data accurately. The dataset contains a few outliers, such as exceptionally high ages due to data entry errors. Which measure of central tendency would be most resistant to the influence of these outliers?
