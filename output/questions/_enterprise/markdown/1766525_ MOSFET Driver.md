## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates MOSFET control, PWM dimming techniques, and microcontroller reliability concepts, ideal for mid-level roles. The problem requires identifying a solution to prevent MCU damage due to overcurrent from the GPIO when controlling an N-channel MOSFET.

## Problem Statement

A project requires a dimmable array of LEDs. An N-channel MOSFET controls the low side current of the LEDs. The LEDs are dimmed using the PWM method. An MCU (microcontroller unit) outputs the PWM signal to the MOSFET gate connected to one of the MCU's GPIOs. While operating at a duty cycle of 50% or more, the MCU is damaged irreparably.

 

Assuming the voltage applied is sufficient, the power supply is correctly sized, and the MOSFET is rated to handle the current, what might be a solution to this problem? 

 

Note: MCU reliability is the primary concern. The MOSFET switching speed is not a concern.
