## Problem Statement

Read the following case study carefully.

 

The University of United States (UUS) offers four year programs in the following courses:

	
- Information Technology
	
- Civil Engineering
	
- Mechanical Engineering 
	
- Electrical Engineering
	
- Fashion Designing
	
- Hotel Management

 

This year, for campus recruitment of senior students, the UUS is planning to call companies needing majors in:

	
- Information Technology (IT)
	
- Mechanical Engineering (ME)
	
- Fashion Designing (FD)

 

They are only calling the best three companies in their fields. For this, they need to record the following details:

	
- Company Name
	
- Company Field
	
- HR Person Name
	
- HR Person Contact
	
- HR Person Email ID
	
- Date of Campus-Visit

 

Also, all of the companies visiting the campus need the following details about the senior students:

	
- Name
	
- Enrollment Number
	
- Enrolled Course
	
- Number of Projects Completed

 

The companies demand the following information about each one of the student's completed projects:

	
- Title
	
- Mentor
	
- Duration
	
- Technology
	
- Grade Awarded

 

UUS has three placement committees: one for each IT, MS and FD focused company. Each committee consists of five students who manage details with the visiting companies. Companies visit campuses with four people including an HR person for interviews. When a company visits the campus, it explains the interview process. Before the interviews, companies always give pre-interview tests to screen students. The tests could be Multiple Choice or Subjective. Sometimes, companies prefer to give both tests. If the student qualifies, then they are called for interviews. Each student has to pass three rounds of interviews. If he passes each round, he has a last interview with the HR person, and the HR person decides whether the student gets selected or not.

 

Draw the use case diagram for this case study.
