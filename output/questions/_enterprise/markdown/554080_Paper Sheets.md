## Summary

This coding question evaluates problem solving, algorithms, and mathematical concepts, ideal for junior-level roles. The task is to determine the minimum number of moves required to fold a rectangular sheet from dimensions h × w to h1 × w1.

## Problem Statement

Given a rectangular sheet with initial dimensions h × w, determine the minimum number of moves required to fold it into a rectangle with dimensions h1 × w1.

	
- A fold can only be made parallel to the edges of the rectangle.
	
- After each fold, the resulting dimensions must be integers.
	
- Folding may be performed along either dimension any number of times.

 

Example

Assume (h, w) = (8, 4), and (h1, w1) = (6, 1).

Output: 3

Explanation:

	
- Fold across the long edge 2 units from one side to create a 6 x 4 rectangle.
	
- Fold across the center of the 4-unit edge to get a 6 x 2 rectangle.
	
- Fold across the center of the 2-unit edge for dimensions of 6 x 1.

 

Constraints

	
- 1 ≤ h, w, h1, w1 ≤ 1015

	
- h1 ≤ h 
	
- w1 ≤ w

Test Case Input Format

There are four lines of input, each with an integer: h, w, h1, and w1, respectively.
