## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates directional reasoning, spatial awareness, and problem-solving concepts, ideal for mid-level roles. The problem requires determining the final direction of a walker after a series of turns.

## Problem Statement

A walker heads east for one kilometer and turns right. After walking another kilometer, the walker turns left, walks a kilometer, turns left, and walks another kilometer. What direction is the walker facing?
