## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates combinatorial reasoning, set theory, and problem-solving concepts, ideal for mid-level roles. The problem requires calculating how many children like to dance based on given preferences for pairs of hobbies among a group of 400 children.

## Problem Statement

In a group of 400 children, they all like exactly two hobbies out of the set singing, dancing, ice skating, and sewing. 100 children like singing and dancing. 70 like singing and ice skating, and 50 like dancing and ice skating. For the remainder, an equal number like all other possible pairs of hobbies.

 

How many like to dance?
