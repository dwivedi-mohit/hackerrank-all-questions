## Summary

This multiple choice question evaluates spatial reasoning, color adjacency, and geometric relationships concepts, ideal for mid-level roles. The problem requires identifying correct statements based on the coloring of a cube's faces.

## Problem Statement

Six faces of a cube are colored in the following manner.

(1) The white face is adjacent to the black face.

(2) The green face is opposite the white face.

(3) The red face is adjacent to the black face.

 

Identify the correct statement(s).
