## Problem Statement

A leading global enterprise, in the midst of transitioning several of its business units, aims to unify various existing platforms into a coherent system. This includes legacy software from some units, modern cloud-based applications from others, and several proprietary data formats that require specialized handling. They have chosen Dell Boomi's AtomSphere for integration. Key priorities include near-real-time data synchronization and cost-effective data transfer. 

The following challenges have been identified:

1. Ensuring seamless communication between systems located in different geographic regions with minimal latency.

2. Efficiently handling, transforming, and routing proprietary data formats.

3. Designing an integration process that scales with the increasing data volume without a proportional increase in cost.

Which of the following action plans is most likely to help achieve the desired outcomes?
