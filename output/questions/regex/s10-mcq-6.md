## Problem Statement

**Objective**	
In this challenge, we're reinforcing what we've learned today. In case you've missed them, today's tutorials are on [Conditional Probability](/challenges/s10-mcq-4/tutorial) and [Combinations and Permutations](/challenges/s10-mcq-5/tutorial).
<br><br>

**Task**	
A bag contains $3$ red marbles and $4$ blue marbles. Then, $2$ marbles are drawn from the bag, at random, without replacement. If the *first* marble drawn is red, what is the probability that the *second* marble is blue?
