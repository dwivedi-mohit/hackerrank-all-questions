## Preview

Predicting $x$ for a given $y$, when given two regression lines.

## Problem Statement

The two regression lines of a bivariate distribution are:

 4$x$ – 5$y$ + 33 = 0  (line of y on x)
 20$x$ – 9$y$ – 107 = 0 (line of x on y).

Estimate the value of $x$ when $y$ = 7.
Compute the correct answer to one decimal place.

**Output Format**

In the text box, enter the floating point/decimal value required. Do not lead any leading or trailing spaces. Your answer may look like:
	7.2
  

This is **NOT** the actual answer - just the format in which you should provide your answer.
