## Preview

Calculate the probability that two dice will roll two unique values having a sum of 6.

## Problem Statement

**Objective**	
In this challenge, we practice calculating probability. We recommend you review the previous challenge's [Tutorial](/challenges/s10-mcq-1/tutorial) before attempting this problem.
<br><br>

**Task**	
In a single toss of $2$ fair (evenly-weighted) six-sided dice, find the probability that the values rolled by each die will be *different* and the two dice have a sum of $6$.
